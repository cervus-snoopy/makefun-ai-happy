WHAT WAS BUILT
---------------
main.py — Python 3.13, standard library ONLY (struct, zlib,
math, pathlib). No Pillow, no numpy, no vendored code, no
external assets, no downloads at runtime.
  - Canvas: 1024x1024 RGB bytearray. Drawing helpers written
    from scratch: fill_rect, fill_ellipse / fill_circle
    (scanline, sqrt per row), fill_polygon (scanline even-odd),
    ring_arc (upper-half annulus for rainbow bands), plus
    heart / flower / butterfly / sparkle shape helpers.
  - PNG encoder written from scratch: 8-bit RGB (colour type 2),
    filter 0 per scanline, zlib level 9, IHDR / IDAT / IEND
    chunks with correct CRC32. This is why no image library is
    needed at runtime.
  - Scene, back to front: (1) sky vertical gradient bright blue
    -> warm pale gold at the horizon; (2) 6-band rainbow centred
    low (red/orange/yellow/green/blue/purple), framing the
    puppy; (3) white clouds; (4) smiling sun top-left with 12
    triangular rays, closed happy eyes, crescent smile, blush;
    (5) rolling green hills; (6) two small birds; (7) two
    butterflies (orange, purple); (8) three floating pink
    hearts; (9) white sparkles; (10) the puppy — floppy ears,
    golden head, forehead tuft, big glossy eyes with white
    highlights, cream muzzle, dark nose with shine, pink tongue,
    blush cheeks, smile curves; (11) six flowers (pink, white,
    purple, yellow) with stems/leaves along the bottom meadow
    plus grass tufts.
  - Output path is exactly /output/entry.png (Path constant at
    the top of main.py); parent directory is created if needed.
  - Determinism: every coordinate and colour is a fixed literal
    in the file. No random, no time, no os.environ, no network,
    no file reads. The only input is the code itself.

WHY THIS IMAGE CONCEPT WAS CHOSEN
----------------------------------
The judge compares images pairwise and picks the happier one, so
the entry must win on first glance at thumbnail size, not on
detail a judge has to study. The concept stacks the most
universal, culturally neutral happiness signals, all in one
frame: a cute animal (golden puppy — the single most reliable
"happy" subject), a smiling sun, a rainbow, warm bright colours
(gold/orange/pink/sky blue/leaf green), flowers, butterflies and
hearts, one large central subject, and a clean uncluttered
composition. Deliberate choices:
  - ONE large central subject (the puppy's face fills ~40% of
    the frame) instead of a busy scene — readable at any size.
  - NO text anywhere: text rendered by a program is the easiest
    way for an image to look wrong (gibberish letters) to a
    vision judge, and the brief does not require text.
  - Warm gradient sky (blue -> gold) instead of flat blue:
    reads as golden-hour happiness, not a cold diagram.
  - Flat vector/cartoon style instead of attempted realism:
    clean shapes cannot fall into uncanny territory, and flat
    colour areas compress to a tiny PNG (23 KB), far inside
    the 1 MiB limit.
  - The leader at build time was "Tea with the foreman" at
    92.4% — a warm, cosy, human scene. This entry answers with
    the other proven warm lane (cute animal + sunshine +
    rainbow) rather than copying the leader's subject.
